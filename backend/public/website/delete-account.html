Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account & Data

You can request the deletion of your GetInRoute account and all associated data at any time. Here's how:

Step 1 — Send a deletion request

Email us at hello@getinroute.com with the subject line "Delete my account". Include the following in your email:

  • Your full name
  • The email address linked to your GetInRoute account
  • Your role (employee, driver, or admin)

Step 2 — What gets deleted

Within 7 business days of receiving your request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your personal information (name, email, phone, address history)
  • Your booking and trip history
  • Your chat messages
  • Your GPS location history
  • Your SafeDrop and SOS records

What we keep

We may retain anonymized, non-identifiable trip data (e.g. aggregate route statistics) for service improvement. This data cannot be linked back to you.

Questions?

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@getinroute.com.
