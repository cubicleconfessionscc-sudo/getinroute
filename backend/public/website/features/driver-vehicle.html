Driver & Vehicle Info

One record per driver and vehicle — documents, assignments, and status at a glance, so nothing expires or gets missed.

What it does

Every driver and vehicle gets a single profile: license and permit details, assigned routes, and current status. Admins can see who's on shift, which vehicle they're driving, and whether any documentation needs renewal.

How it helps

  • Store license, permit, and insurance details in one place
  • See which driver is assigned to which route at a glance
  • Get reminded before a document is due to expire
  • Track vehicle maintenance status alongside assignments

Built for

Fleet and compliance teams who need documentation and assignments to stay current without manual chasing.
