Pickup & Drop Management

Assign employees to the right stops, adjust pickup and drop points on the fly, and keep every roster accurate without back-and-forth spreadsheets.

What it does

This module lets admins map every employee to a pickup and drop-off point, group riders by zone, and reassign stops in seconds when someone's address or shift changes. Every roster stays a live, editable list instead of a static export.

How it helps

  • Bulk-assign employees to stops by zone or shift
  • Move a rider between routes without rebuilding the trip
  • See stop-level headcounts before a route goes out
  • Keep a change history so nothing gets lost between shifts

Built for

HR and transport coordinators managing rosters that shift week to week — new joiners, address changes, and temporary stop changes.
