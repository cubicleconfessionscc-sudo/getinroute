GetInRoute helps organizations run employee transportation end-to-end — planning commutes, assigning cabs, and tracking every trip live.

For employees
• See your cab move live on a map as it heads to your pickup and home stops
• Get arrival, delay, and route-change alerts the moment they happen
• Chat or call your driver with one tap
• View your full trip history

For drivers
• Receive your daily trip roster with pickup order and stops
• Share your live location with riders and admins during a trip
• Update trip status with quick, one-tap replies

For admins
• Plan shifts and recurring commutes ahead of time
• Assign cabs, drivers, and routes from one dashboard
• Track every trip in real time
• Manage drivers, vehicles, vendors, and daily allotments

Who it's for
GetInRoute is built for companies that run employee transportation, and for the people who ride every day — simpler planning, fewer missed pickups, safer journeys.

Background location explanation (paste in the "Location permissions" / background access field):
```
GetInRoute lets a driver share their live position during an active commute so employees and admins can track the cab on a map. The background location permission is used ONLY while a trip is active and the driver has explicitly enabled "share live location". Live position is not collected at any other time, and riders do not have their device location tracked in the background. Users can revoke location permission at any time in device settings.
```
